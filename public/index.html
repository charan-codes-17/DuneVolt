Prototype Architecture & Claims Honesty Guide

Compliance with PRD Section 13 (Real vs Simulated AI Split) & Section 16 (Claims Guidelines)
Transparency Notice: DuneVolt is an interactive digital twin built for judging demonstration. To maintain rigorous technical honesty, this prototype explicitly separates physics-based mathematical models from rule-based AI simulations.
⚙️ Computed with Real Math & Physics
  • Sun Trigonometry & Tracking: Sun elevation angle drives optimal panel tilt ($\theta = 90^\circ - \text{sunAngle}$) dynamically.
  • Thermal Derating Loss: Standard $-0.4\%/^\circ\text{C}$ temperature derate applied above $25^\circ\text{C}$ STC baseline.
  • Dust Soiling Attenuation: Optical transmission derating calculated directly from particulate concentration.
  • Loss Attribution Arithmetic: Panel output vs expected baseline calculated across all active arrays.
  • Energy Balance Routing: $\text{Generation} = \text{Grid Export} + \text{Battery Charge} + \text{Shared Community Solar}$.
🤖 Simulated & Rule-Based AI Engine
  • Storm Risk Prediction: Multi-sensor weighted risk composite (wind, dust, visibility thresholds).
  • Compressed Time-Scale: 10-second storm countdown simulates a real-world 25-minute radar horizon.
  • 7-Factor Root Cause Attribution: Multi-factor diagnostic isolation (dust, thermal, hotspot, wiring, crack, shading, degradation).
  • Visual & Thermal CV Findings: High-resolution optical/thermal scan report generated from sensor telemetry.
  • Condition-Based Maintenance: Prioritized cleaning queue ranked by economic revenue loss (₹) vs dispatch cost.

Product Vision & Positioning

DuneVolt proves that centralized, AI-managed desert solar can absorb the extreme maintenance and operational burden of harsh desert environments — providing a scalable, resilient alternative that reduces community dependence on universal rooftop solar adoption.
